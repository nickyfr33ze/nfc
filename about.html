About

Who I Am

I'm an IT consultant, systems engineer, and security researcher based in Eugene, Oregon. You found NFC, a solo consultancy focused on practical IT support, automation, backend tooling, and security foundations for small teams and departments.

I hold a Bachelor's in Business Administration with a minor in Computer Science. My background spans endpoint support, server administration, Python scripting, security research, and building internal tools that solve real problems.

Why NFC

Most IT consulting shops are built for enterprises. They sell SLAs, dedicated accounts, and 50-page proposals. That works for a Fortune 500 company. It doesn't work for a department of 15 people who just need their systems to work.

NFC exists for the teams who need competent, direct help without the overhead. You get a single operator who understands the full stack, from endpoint to server to script to security, and who ships scoped work on time.

No fluff. No scope creep. Just working infrastructure.

What I Work On

  • Systems & Infrastructure - macOS, Windows, Linux. Server maintenance, monitoring, documentation, user support.
  • Automation - Python, Bash, PowerShell. Killing manual processes and building tools that save recurring time.
  • Backend Development - Web tools, API integrations, internal applications. Practical software that solves actual problems.
  • Security - Defensive foundations, hardening, awareness. Lab-driven research turned into actionable recommendations.

Other Interests

When not working on client projects, I contribute to open-source tools, research security topics, and explore the intersection of technology and personal development. These interests inform my approach to consulting; practical, research-driven, and always focused on what actually works.
